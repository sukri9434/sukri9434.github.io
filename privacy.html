Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

ARA Autopost is a personal tool used by its owner. This policy explains what the app accesses when the owner authorizes it with TikTok.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the app uses the TikTok scopes user.info.basic (to identify the connected account) and video.upload or video.publish (to send the owner's own videos to that account).

Data stored

The app stores only the OAuth tokens needed to keep the connection working, in encrypted GitHub Actions secrets. It does not store viewer data, does not collect data about other TikTok users, and does not sell or share any data with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the connection is active. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app, and the owner can delete the stored tokens at any time. To request deletion, email sukri9434@gmail.com.
